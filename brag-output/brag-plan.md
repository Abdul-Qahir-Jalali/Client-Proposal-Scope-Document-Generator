# Brag Plan: Client Proposal & Scope Document Generator

## What is this app?
A Streamlit app that turns a few project details into a finished client proposal (summary, scope, timeline, pricing, next steps) that you can edit live and export to HTML, Word or PDF, with no API key and no internet needed to generate it.

## The angle
A quiet, premium product film: "details in, proposal out". It shows the real working flow (type four facts, press Generate) and then the real output (the photo cover, the sourced quote, the timeline, the price section), cut to the beat. Nothing is mocked up except the input form; every proposal screen is a real render of the app's output for the bundled sample "Bright Dental Clinic: website redesign" (a fictional client).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The headline "A few project details." appears while a form fills in by typing: Client, Project type, Timeline, Pricing.

## Key moments
- The finished cover: a relevant photo, the client name at poster size, the phase band.
- The opening quote with the person's name and source.
- The timeline drawn as bars from the phases.
- The price section with the total as the largest figure.

## Outro / punchline
Export to HTML, Word or PDF (three pills landing on the beat), then the product name and "No API key. No internet to generate a proposal."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: four fields typed into the form. Key action: press "Generate proposal". Result: the finished proposal page, scrolled section by section.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with app-store cleanliness)
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: few scenes, confident holds, restrained sound, motion that decelerates and never bounces.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24 seconds

- Strongest visual element: the photo cover and the navy Investment section with the tangerine total

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (Sascha Ende, ende.app, CC BY 4.0), about 110 BPM
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume 0.5, fades out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Scene cuts snap to beats 4.10, 8.22, 11.47, 14.73; strong cues 18.01 and 20.74 carry the export reveal and the outro title.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (extraction helper not installed in this run; documented, not blocking)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, warm and low high-frequency risk (key presses for typing, a click for the button, soft impacts on the two strong cues, small clicks for the pills)
- Restraint rule: SFX stay under the music; no stingers on every cut.
