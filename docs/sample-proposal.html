Executive summary

Allure Digital will plan, build and deliver a website redesign for Bright Dental Clinic over 8 weeks. The key goals are to increase online appointment bookings by 30%, modernise the clinic's look and build patient trust and make the site fast and easy to use on phones. The work covers 5 key deliverables, including 6-page responsive website (Home, Services, Team, Pricing, Reviews, Contact) and online appointment booking integration. The estimated investment is $6,500 – $8,000. This document sets out the scope, timeline and pricing so both sides agree on the work before it starts.

Scope of work

What is included, and what is not.

Included

  • 6-page responsive website (Home, Services, Team, Pricing, Reviews, Contact)
  • Online appointment booking integration
  • Patient testimonials page
  • Basic on-page SEO and Google Business setup
  • 30 days of post-launch support
  • Two rounds of revisions per deliverable
  • Regular progress updates

Not included

  • Writing website copy and taking photography (unless listed above)
  • Domain registration and hosting fees
  • Paid plug-ins, stock images and third-party licences
  • Work on pages or features not listed in the deliverables

Assumptions

  • The client names one main contact who can give feedback and approvals.
  • Feedback is shared within 3 working days. Late feedback may move the timeline.
  • Content, logins and files the client agreed to supply arrive on time.
  • Requests outside the scope above are quoted and agreed in writing before work starts.
